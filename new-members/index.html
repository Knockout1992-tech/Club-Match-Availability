WELCOME TO THE CLUB

New to rugby?
Let's get you started.

We're looking forward to welcoming you to Rushden & Higham Mini Section. Here's everything you need to know to get started.

Important – please read before registering

Before your child's first training session, a parent or guardian must create their own adult RFU GMS account.

Do not create a separate GMS account for your child. Once your adult account is set up, link your child to it. This is how you will access and manage their player record and membership.

This is required for the RFU's player registration and insurance arrangements.

Part 1 – Set up your RFU GMS account

Please follow these steps in order. The adult creates the account first, then adds their child.

1

Create your adult account

A parent or guardian must create their own GMS account using their personal details. Please don't create the account in your child's name.

2

Link your child – don't create another account

Once you're logged in, add or link your child to your existing adult account. Do not create a separate account or login for your child. Linking their record is how you'll view and manage their player registration.

3

Find Rushden & Higham RUFC

Use Find a Club and search for Rushden & Higham RUFC.

4

Favourite our club ♥

Add Rushden & Higham RUFC to your favourites. This makes it easier to find us again when you return to GMS.

Open GMS – create your adult account ↗ Already have an account? Log in ↗

If you already have a GMS account, please log in to that account rather than creating another one. You can then link your child and find our club.

YOUR FIRST FOUR WEEKS

Enjoy your four-week trial!

Come along, meet the coaches and enjoy playing rugby! Our Mini Section offers new players a four-week trial so they can get to know the team and try the sport.

✓

After four weeks – apply for membership

Once your child's trial period is complete, log back into GMS and apply for their club membership.
