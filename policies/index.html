1. Privacy notice

Data controller: Loading current club wording…

Why we use information. Rushden & Higham Rugby Union Football Club uses Club Check-In and the Mini Section Parent Portal to run and administer its mini rugby activities. This includes maintaining player and age-group records, recording attendance, planning training sessions and fixtures, asking parents/carers about availability, communicating relevant event information, managing family access to responses and protecting the club's systems. We use information only for legitimate club, rugby, safeguarding, safety, administration and legal purposes connected with those activities.

This notice applies to information processed through both applications and the related database, hosting and security services. It does not replace any separate privacy information the club may provide for other activities.

Information we may hold

  • Child's name, age group and, where provided, school year.
  • Attendance at training sessions and other club activities.
  • Match and event availability, including available, unavailable or unsure responses.
  • Parent or carer's name and account/PIN verification information.
  • Family-sharing links and records of which parent/carer submitted or updated a response.
  • Submission and update dates, fixture details and relevant system/security records.
  • Technical information generated by the services used to operate and protect the system, which may include device, browser, network or access-log information depending on supplier configuration.

Parent Portal accounts and responses. A parent/carer enters their name and creates or uses a six-digit PIN to access the portal. The PIN is used to verify the account and is stored as a salted, one-way verification hash; it is not stored as readable text. The PIN should be kept private and must not be shared. The portal does not currently ask for a parent's telephone number or email address, and the optional parent-contact field is not populated by the application.

For each child included in a response, the portal collects the child's full name, selected age group, optional school year and the parent's availability choice (available, unavailable or unsure) for a particular fixture or event. It also records information needed to manage and update the response, such as submission and update times and internal record references. Parents can return to view and update responses while the relevant response remains open for changes.

Family sharing. A parent may invite another parent/carer to link their accounts so that they can view and manage the relevant shared family fixture responses. Invitations use a one-time link which expires after 24 hours. The system records family membership and invitation status so that access can be managed, cancelled or ended. Only invite someone who is authorised to see and manage the relevant family information.

Club Check-In records. Club Check-In holds player names, age-group information and attendance records for club activities. Coaches use the information needed for the age groups they are authorised to manage; authorised administrators manage wider club records and access.

Technical and security information. The services record information needed to authenticate access, protect accounts, manage submissions, investigate errors and maintain security. Depending on the event, this may include account or player references, attempted names, event outcomes, timestamps, installation or device identifiers and technical/network information generated by the hosting or database providers. PINs, PIN hashes and authentication tokens must not be included in audit records.

The portal does not currently ask parents to provide children's medical information or other special-category information as part of routine attendance or availability. If the club introduces a feature that requires such information, it must assess the lawful basis, any additional legal condition, necessity, safeguards and privacy information before collecting it.

Information about children is normally provided by their parent/carer. The club will make privacy information available to parents/carers and explain the use of information to children in a way that is appropriate to their age and understanding.

Who can see information?

Player names, attendance records and availability responses are not published as public information. Coaches can access the information needed for the age groups they are authorised to manage, including the relevant player and availability information. They must not use it for unrelated purposes or share it outside the club without authorisation.

Authorised administrators can access wider club information and manage records and user access where required for their responsibilities. System administrators may access or process information when maintaining the service, applying security controls or investigating technical or security issues.

Parents/carers can view and manage the relevant information and responses associated with their own portal account. Where a family-sharing link has been accepted, the linked parent/carer can also access the shared family responses. A fixture link may display fixture or event details needed to submit a response; it does not make parent names, child names or submitted availability publicly visible.

Access is controlled through the relevant parent account/PIN or club access arrangements. Access should be removed or changed when a parent link, coaching role or administrative responsibility ends.

Lawful basis and sharing

Lawful basis. The club's usual lawful basis for the routine administration described in this notice is legitimate interests: the club has a legitimate interest in organising rugby activities, managing teams, confirming attendance and availability, administering fixtures, supporting safe participation and protecting its systems. The club must balance those interests against the rights and reasonable expectations of players and families, with particular care where information relates to children. The club limits the information collected, restricts access by role and provides ways for individuals to exercise their rights.

Legitimate interests will not automatically be the appropriate basis for every activity. If the purpose or type of information changes, the club will identify and document the appropriate lawful basis before processing it. If special-category information is ever required, an additional condition under data protection law will also be needed. The club does not rely on consent as a blanket basis for the routine operation of the portal.

Sharing information. Information may be made available to authorised coaches and club administrators where needed for their roles. It may also be processed by service providers who host, store, deliver, secure or support the applications. The club may disclose information where necessary for safeguarding, an emergency, the prevention or investigation of misuse, or to meet a legal obligation. The club does not sell personal information or use it for unrelated marketing.

How long information is kept

The club keeps information only for as long as it is needed for the purpose for which it was collected, taking account of ongoing club administration, safeguarding, insurance, legal and regulatory requirements. Your rights

Parents/carers and, where appropriate, players may ask the club to confirm whether it processes their personal information and request a copy. They may also ask for inaccurate information to be corrected, request erasure or restriction where the legal conditions are met, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. The right to data portability applies only in specified circumstances. These rights are not absolute and the club may need to retain or continue using some information where the law permits or requires it, for example for safeguarding, legal claims or statutory obligations. You can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

How we protect information

The club uses role-based access arrangements, parent PIN verification, restricted database access and security logging to help protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. Parent PINs are verified using one-way hashes rather than stored as readable PINs. Access to sensitive parent and family records is handled through the portal's protected server-side functions rather than being made available as unrestricted public database access. The club and its providers may use technical and organisational measures such as secure hosting, access controls, backups, monitoring and security updates. No online service can be guaranteed completely secure, so users must also protect their PINs and report suspected misuse promptly.

2. Acceptable use policy

This policy applies to coaches, administrators and system administrators who access Club Check-In or the protected areas of the Mini Section Parent Portal. It also sets expectations for parents/carers using their portal accounts and any family-sharing feature.

  • Access is personal and must only be used by the person it was issued to. Parent/carer accounts and PINs must not be shared with another person; use the family-sharing invitation feature where another parent/carer needs access.
  • Coaches must use player and attendance information only for legitimate coaching and club administration purposes, and only to the extent needed for their role. They must not access, use or share information out of curiosity or for personal purposes.
  • Administrators and system administrators may use the information and controls required for their authorised club responsibilities.
  • PINs, passwords, invitation links and other access credentials must not be shared or reused by another person.
  • Information must only be viewed or used for a legitimate club purpose. Parents/carers must only access information relating to their own family or information shared with them through an authorised family link.
  • Player or parent information must not be copied, downloaded, photographed, screenshotted or circulated unless there is a genuine need and the club has authorised it.
  • Personal copies of registers or availability lists must not be retained indefinitely or stored in unsecured locations.
  • Information must not be used for personal purposes, marketing or unrelated contact.

  • Access must be reviewed and removed when a person leaves their coaching or administrative role or no longer needs access. Family-sharing links should be cancelled or ended when they are no longer appropriate.
  • The club may review access and system activity for security, audit and investigation purposes.

Access may be suspended while a suspected breach or misuse is investigated. Users must not attempt to bypass access controls, guess another person's PIN, use another person's account or share screenshots or exports containing personal information in public groups or on social media.

Deletion and backups

When information is deleted from active systems, copies held in secure backups may remain until they are overwritten in accordance with the relevant provider's backup arrangements. Information retained for a safeguarding, insurance, legal or incident-related reason is restricted to that purpose and reviewed when the reason for retention ends.

4. Data protection responsibilities

The club committee is accountable for the club's decisions about why and how personal information is used, including approval of this policy, risk decisions and appropriate resources.

Data incidents

Any suspected personal data breach must be reported to the club promptly. The club records what happened, what information and people were affected, what action was taken and whether notification to the ICO or affected individuals is required. Where notification to the ICO is required, the legal time limit is generally 72 hours from awareness of the breach.

6. Review, approval and contact

    The club maintains this policy, its retention arrangements, access arrangements and supplier information as part of its data protection responsibilities. The club reviews the information it collects when features, purposes, access roles or suppliers change. Where a new activity is likely to result in a high risk to individuals, the club completes a Data Protection Impact Assessment before that activity begins. A review should also consider whether the legitimate interests assessment remains appropriate and whether the information given to parents and children is still accurate.
