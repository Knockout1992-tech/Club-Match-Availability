1. Privacy notice

Data controller: Loading current club wording…

Why we use information. We use Club Check-In and the Mini Section Parent Portal to organise and administer our mini rugby activities. This includes keeping player and age-group records, recording attendance, planning training sessions and fixtures, asking parents and carers about availability, sharing relevant event information, managing family access to responses and protecting our systems. We use personal information for club and rugby administration, safeguarding, safety and other lawful purposes connected with running the mini section.

This notice explains how we handle information through these two applications and the database, hosting and security services that support them. Other club activities may have their own privacy information.

Information we may hold

  • Child's name, age group and, where provided, school year.
  • Attendance at training sessions and other club activities.
  • Match and event availability, including available, unavailable or unsure responses.
  • Parent or carer's name and account/PIN verification information.
  • Family-sharing links and records of which parent/carer submitted or updated a response.
  • Submission and update dates, fixture details and relevant system/security records.
  • Technical information generated by the services used to operate and protect the system, which may include device, browser, network or access-log information depending on supplier configuration.
  • Adult first-aider volunteer names and their main/additional age-group allocations, including optional pitch information, where used to coordinate first-aid cover.

Parent Portal accounts and responses. When using the Parent Portal, a parent or carer enters their name and creates or uses a six-digit PIN. The PIN verifies the account and is stored as a salted, one-way verification hash, not as readable text. Please keep your PIN private and do not share it. The portal does not currently ask parents or carers for a telephone number or email address, and the optional parent-contact field is not populated by the application.

For each child included in a response, we collect their full name, selected age group, optional school year and the parent's or carer's availability choice (available, unavailable or unsure) for that fixture or event. The portal also records the information needed to manage the response, such as when it was submitted or updated and its internal record reference. Parents and carers can return to view and update a response while it remains open.

Family sharing. You may invite another parent or carer to link their accounts so they can view and manage the relevant shared family fixture responses. Each invitation uses a one-time link that expires after 24 hours. The system records family membership and invitation status so we can manage, cancel or end access. Please only invite someone who is authorised to see and manage that family information.

Remember Me and passkey sign-in

On a return visit, the app pre-fills the remembered name and automatically attempts passkey authentication if a passkey is registered. Passkeys use the device's own security features, such as fingerprint, facial recognition or screen lock. Biometric information is handled by the device and is not sent to the club through Club Check-In. If passkey authentication is unavailable or unsuccessful, the user can still sign in using their personal PIN. Remember Me does not keep users permanently signed in or bypass the existing session security and timeout controls. Users can untick Remember Me to remove their saved name from that device.

Club Check-In records. We use Club Check-In to keep player names, age-group information and attendance records for club activities. Coaches can see the information needed for the age groups they are authorised to manage. Authorised administrators manage wider club records and access.

Technical and security information. The applications and supporting services record information needed to verify access, protect accounts, manage submissions, investigate errors and maintain security. Depending on the event, this may include account or player references, names entered during an attempted action, event outcomes, timestamps, installation or device identifiers and technical or network information generated by our hosting or database providers. We do not include PINs, PIN hashes or authentication tokens in our audit records.

Coach push notifications. Coaches can choose to enable push notifications on a supported device to receive relevant club event and fixture updates. If enabled, the application stores a device-specific push subscription, including the technical details needed to route notifications. Notifications are delivered through the browser or device push service, which may process subscription and delivery information. We limit notification messages to relevant club updates and avoid including unnecessary personal information about children or families. Coaches can switch notifications off in Club Check-In or through their device or browser settings.

The portal does not currently ask parents or carers to provide children's medical information or other special-category information as part of routine attendance or availability. If we consider introducing a feature that needs this type of information, we will first assess whether it is necessary, identify the appropriate lawful basis and any additional legal condition, put suitable safeguards in place and update our privacy information before collecting it.

Parents and carers normally provide information about their children. We make this privacy information available to families and will explain how we use information to children in a way that is appropriate to their age and understanding.

Who can see information?

We do not publish player names, attendance records or availability responses as public information. Coaches can access the information needed for the age groups they are authorised to manage, including relevant player and availability information. They must only use it for their club role and must not share it outside the club without authorisation.

Our authorised administrators can access wider club information and manage records and user access where their responsibilities require it. System administrators may access or process information when maintaining the service, applying security controls or investigating technical or security issues. This includes access to first-aider volunteer names and their age-group/pitch allocations for first-aid cover coordination. First-aider volunteer records are restricted to the System Admin function and are not shown to Parents or Coaches.

Parents and carers can view and manage the information and responses associated with their own portal account. If a family-sharing invitation has been accepted, the linked parent or carer can also access the shared family responses. A fixture link displays the fixture or event details needed to submit a response; it does not make parent names, child names or submitted availability publicly visible.

We control access through the relevant parent account and PIN or the club's access arrangements. We remove or change access when a family link, coaching role or administrative responsibility ends.

Lawful basis and sharing

Lawful basis. For the routine administration described in this notice, we generally rely on legitimate interests. We have a legitimate interest in organising rugby activities, managing teams, confirming attendance and availability, administering fixtures, supporting safe participation and protecting our systems. We consider these interests alongside the rights and reasonable expectations of players and families, taking particular care because much of the information relates to children. We keep the information we collect to what we need, restrict access according to people's roles and explain how individuals can exercise their rights.

Legitimate interests will not be the right lawful basis for every activity. If the purpose or type of information we use changes, we will identify and document the appropriate lawful basis before using it. If we ever need special-category information, we will also identify an additional condition under data protection law. We do not rely on consent as a blanket basis for the routine operation of the portal.

Sharing information. We make information available to authorised coaches and club administrators when their roles require it. Our service providers may also process information to host, store, deliver, secure or support the applications. We may share information where necessary for safeguarding, an emergency, preventing or investigating misuse, or meeting a legal obligation. We do not sell personal information or use it for unrelated marketing.

How long information is kept

We keep information only for as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected, taking account of club administration, safeguarding, insurance, legal and regulatory requirements. Your rights

You, and players where appropriate, can ask us to confirm whether we use your personal information and request a copy. You can also ask us to correct inaccurate information, request erasure or restriction where the legal conditions are met, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. The right to data portability applies only in certain circumstances. These rights are not absolute: we may need to keep or continue using some information where the law permits or requires it, for example for safeguarding, legal claims or statutory obligations. We will respond within the applicable legal time limits and explain if we cannot meet a request in full. Loading current club contact wording…

You can also ask us to review access through a family-sharing link or remove a link that is no longer appropriate. Ending a family link does not automatically erase the underlying fixture or attendance records; those records may still need to be kept under our retention schedule.

You can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

How we protect information

We use role-based access, parent PIN verification, restricted database access and security logging to help protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. Parent PINs are verified using one-way hashes; they are not stored as readable PINs. Access to sensitive parent and family records is handled through the portal's protected server-side functions, rather than through unrestricted public database access. We and our service providers use technical and organisational measures such as secure hosting, access controls, backups, monitoring and security updates. No online service can be guaranteed completely secure, so please protect your PIN and tell us promptly if you suspect it has been misused.

2. Acceptable use policy

This policy explains the standards we expect everyone to follow when using Club Check-In or the protected areas of the Mini Section Parent Portal. It applies to coaches, administrators, system administrators and parents or carers using portal accounts or family-sharing features.

  • Your access is personal and must only be used by you. Do not share your parent or carer account or PIN with another person. Use the family-sharing invitation feature when another parent or carer needs access.
  • As coaches, we use player and attendance information only for genuine coaching and club administration purposes, and only to the extent needed for our roles. We do not access, use or share information out of curiosity or for personal reasons.
  • Administrators and system administrators use only the information and controls needed to carry out their authorised club responsibilities.
  • Keep PINs, passwords, invitation links and other access details private. Do not pass them to another person.
  • Only view or use information for a genuine club purpose. Parents and carers should access information relating to their own family, or information shared with them through an authorised family link.
  • Do not copy, download, photograph, screenshot or circulate player or parent information unless there is a genuine need and the club has authorised it.
  • Do not keep personal copies of registers or availability lists indefinitely or store them somewhere that is not secure.
  • Do not use club information for personal purposes, marketing or unrelated contact.

  • We review and remove access when someone leaves a coaching or administrative role or no longer needs it. Family-sharing links should be cancelled or ended when they are no longer appropriate.
  • We may review access and system activity for security, audit and investigation purposes.

We may suspend access while we investigate suspected misuse or a security breach. Do not try to bypass access controls, guess another person's PIN, use another person's account or share screenshots or exports containing personal information in public groups or on social media.

Deletion and backups

When we delete information from active systems, copies in secure backups may remain until they are overwritten under the relevant provider's backup arrangements. We restrict information kept for safeguarding, insurance, legal or incident-related reasons to that purpose and review it when the reason for keeping it ends.

4. Data protection responsibilities

Our club committee is responsible for decisions about why and how we use personal information. This includes approving this policy, considering data protection risks and making sure appropriate resources are available.

RoleResponsibility

Data incidents

Please report any suspected personal data breach to the club promptly using the contact details in this policy. We record what happened, whose information may have been affected, what action we took and whether we need to notify the ICO or the people affected. Where notification to the ICO is required, the legal time limit is generally 72 hours from the time we become aware of the breach.

6. Review, approval and contact

If a new activity is likely to create a high risk to individuals, we complete a Data Protection Impact Assessment before it begins. We also review whether our legitimate interests assessment remains appropriate and whether the information we give to parents, carers and children is still accurate.
